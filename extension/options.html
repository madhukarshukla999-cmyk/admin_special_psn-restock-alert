💳 Card details for instant checkout

Paytm usually has no saved cards, so enter the card once here and the extension fills the form for you. It never presses Pay – you press Pay and enter the bank OTP.

That takes you to your bank's OTP page. Nothing is charged until you enter the OTP.

⚠ These details are stored only in this browser profile, unencrypted. Anyone who can use this computer could read them. Leaving CVV empty is safer: nobody can pay with the card without it. They're wiped if you revoke this browser from Telegram.

👀 Check GamesTheShop from this browser

Checks every PlayStation card on GamesTheShop from your own internet connection and opens BUY NOW the moment one comes back (after its product page confirms the stock). One small request per check, without your GamesTheShop login. Only works while this browser is open. Turn it on in one browser only.

👀 Check Amazon from this browser

Checks these Amazon cards from your own internet connection every few seconds and opens Buy Now the moment one comes back, usually well before the bot's alert arrives. The pages are loaded without your Amazon login. Only works while this browser is open. Turn it on in one browser only.
